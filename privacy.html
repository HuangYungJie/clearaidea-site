Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Overview

ClearAIdea is an AI-powered project planning app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your ideas, tasks, phases, and notes are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or store this content on our servers.

Account & Sign-In

AI features (generating plans and AI chat) require signing in with Apple or Google. When you sign in, we receive a unique account identifier and, depending on the provider and your choices, your email address. We use this only to secure your account, link your subscription, and apply fair-use limits — never for advertising.

Data Sent to AI Services

When you generate a plan or use AI chat, the content of your request is sent to our server and on to the AI provider to generate a response. This content is processed in real time and is not stored on our servers or used to train AI models.

Usage & Rate Limiting

To prevent abuse and enforce free and paid limits, we store a randomly generated device identifier and per-account usage counts on our server. These do not contain any content from your ideas or chats.

Subscriptions

Paid subscriptions ("ClearAIdea Pro") are processed through the Apple App Store and managed via RevenueCat, which receives your account identifier and subscription status to unlock Pro features. We do not receive your full payment card details.

What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name or contacts, we do not use advertising or third-party tracking/analytics SDKs, and we do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising. We do not store the content of your ideas or chats on our servers.

Third-Party Services

ClearAIdea relies on: Supabase (account authentication and as a secure relay for AI requests), Google Gemini (to generate AI responses), RevenueCat (to manage subscriptions), and Apple and Google (as sign-in providers). Their respective privacy policies apply to data they process.

Data Retention & Account Deletion

Content stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the app. Account data held on our servers (your account identifier, your email if provided, and usage counts) is retained while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and all of its server-side data at any time directly in the app under Settings → Account → Delete Account. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete server-side account data.

Children's Privacy

ClearAIdea is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

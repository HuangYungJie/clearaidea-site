Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

Overview

ClearAIdea is an AI-powered project planning app. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data Stored on Your Device

Your ideas, tasks, phases, and notes are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or store this data on our servers.

Data Sent to AI Services

When you use the AI chat or generate a plan, the content of your request is sent to our server to generate a response. This data is processed in real time and is not stored or used for training.

What We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your name, email, or any personal identifiers. We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not share your data with third parties for advertising. We do not store your ideas or chat history on our servers.

Third-Party Services

ClearAIdea uses Supabase to process AI requests as a relay, and Google Gemini API to generate AI responses. Their respective privacy policies apply to data processed by these services.

Children's Privacy

ClearAIdea is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Data Retention

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
